# A bullet followed by spaces and a tab

What follows the marker is only spaces and tabs, so the item is empty and
cannot interrupt the paragraph. The line's final spaces and tab are removed
from the paragraph's content.

docs/adjudications/0005-closing-draft-0s-gaps.md, item 5:

> **5. An empty list item** is one whose first line after the marker
> holds only spaces and tabs, CommonMark's blank. A form feed is not
> blank.
