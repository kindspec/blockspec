# A heading whose slug comes out empty

The empty name does not reach the heading, and neither does its text.

docs/adjudications/0005-closing-draft-0s-gaps.md, item 8:

> **8. A heading whose slug comes out empty has no slug**, as `# !!!`
> has none. A reference to it is `#REF!(name)`, and so is the empty
> name.

SPEC.md, §8.2:

> and a heading whose slug comes out empty, such as `# !!!`, has none
> (0005 item 8). A reference to either is `#REF!(name)`.
