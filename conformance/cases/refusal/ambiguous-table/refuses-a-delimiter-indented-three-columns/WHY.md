# A pipeless header over a delimiter indented three columns

Three columns is still a delimiter line, so this is a candidate with no
unescaped pipe in its header.

docs/adjudications/0005-closing-draft-0s-gaps.md, item 2:

> **2. A table's delimiter line** indented four or more columns is not a
> delimiter line. It continues the paragraph above.

SPEC.md, §4.3:

> A candidate with no unescaped pipe in one of its two lines is refused
> as `ambiguous-table`.
