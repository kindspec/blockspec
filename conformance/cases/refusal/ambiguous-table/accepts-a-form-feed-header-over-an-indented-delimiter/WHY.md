# A form feed over an indented line

The form-feed rule applies to a delimiter line and the paragraph line over
it. This line is not a delimiter line, so the rule does not reach it.

docs/adjudications/0005-closing-draft-0s-gaps.md, item 2:

> **2. A table's delimiter line** indented four or more columns is not a
> delimiter line. It continues the paragraph above.

SPEC.md, §4.3:

> A delimiter line and the paragraph line over it, in the same
> container, are judged as two lines
