READING: 0002 states the table rule twice, as two refused shapes (a paragraph
line above, a non-blank line below) and as "a table must have a blank line, or
its container's edge, on both sides"; the brief says "a table touching a
non-blank line above or below is refused". This tree takes the general
statement. It is the narrower profile, and 0002 §3 is the precedent for
starting narrow: relaxing a refusal later is lossless.
