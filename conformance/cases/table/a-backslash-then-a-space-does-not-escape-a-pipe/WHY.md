# A backslash that is not directly before the pipe

Control for the double-backslash cases. A space sits between the backslash
and the pipe, so the pipe is unescaped and splits the cell.

docs/adjudications/0005-closing-draft-0s-gaps.md, item 1:

> A `|` is escaped when the character directly before it is a backslash,
> however many backslashes come before that one.
